Data Safety, Sync & Backups

Your data is stored 100% locally on your browser using IndexedDB. Export your file to move data across devices or store safe offline backups.

Moving Between Desktop & Phone?

1. Tap Export Backup (JSON) on Device A.
2. Send or transfer the file to Device B.
3. Open Winter Arc on Device B and click Import Backup File.
All progress, streaks, habits, and reflections will restore instantly!
